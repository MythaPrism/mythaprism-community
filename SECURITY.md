# Security & privacy reports

If you believe you have found a security or privacy issue in MythaPrism (the web tool, the apps, the website or the
account system), please **email hello@mythaprism.com** with the subject line `SECURITY` instead of opening a public
issue. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and how we can reach you. We aim to acknowledge within 3 business
days and to fix confirmed issues before they are discussed publicly. Please do not access other people's data, and do
not test against the payment system with real cards.

Your photos never leave your device, the account system stores only your email and which plan it unlocked. See the
[privacy policy](https://mythaprism.com/privacy.html).
