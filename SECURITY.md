# Security and privacy reports

If you think you found a security or privacy problem in MythaPrism (the web tool, the apps, the website or the account system), please do not open a public issue. Write to **support@mythaprism.com** with the word SECURITY in the subject. Tell us what you found, how to see it again, and how we can reach you.

We answer as soon as we can, and we fix confirmed problems before anyone talks about them in public. Please do not look at other people's data, and do not test payments with real cards.

Your photos never leave your device. What we do keep is listed in the [Privacy Policy](https://mythaprism.com/privacy.html).
